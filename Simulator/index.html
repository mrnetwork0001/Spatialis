Real - the voice parser, surface anchor engine and material swapper are the shipped Lens scripts, running unchanged.
Simulated - the room and its surfaces, head pose (arrow keys), pinch (mouse or touch), speech (Web Speech), display (three.js).
The dashed table is the room's own, standing in for a real table in your space. Clear the room removes only what you placed.
What runs where

VoiceCommandController, SurfaceAnchorEngine and PBRMaterialSwapper run as instances of the shipped Scripts/*.ts: parsing, placement, materials, spawn animation, the repeated-speech filter, feedback.

Stand-ins for hardware: the room and its hit tests (World Query on device), head pose from the arrow keys (headset tracking), drag and scroll as pinch and scale (Spectacles Interaction Kit hand tracking; SpatialGestureController does not run here and is covered by tests), Web Speech for the speech module, three.js for the display.
